How Customer Referral Works
  • When a new client signs up using your referral link, they are permanently linked to your profile as their attributing Team Member.
  • All plot reservations initiated by attributed customers will reflect in your Sales Pipeline and contribute towards your performance incentives.
  • You can also book plots on their behalf anytime via the Book a Plot screen.
