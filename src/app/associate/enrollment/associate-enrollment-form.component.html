6Terms & Conditions

Commission Rules

Rule 1 — Member Sales: If a Team Member successfully sells a plot, they will receive a direct 5% commission on their sale. On this specific sale, the Team's Associate (Leader) will receive 0.5% (as Passive Income).

Rule 2 — Associate Sales: If the Associate (Leader) themselves sells a plot, they will receive a direct 5% commission on their sale.

10-Team-Member Rule: On this sale, the remaining 10 Team Members will each receive 0.5% commission. On any specific Team Member's sale, the Associate receives 0.5%, and each of the remaining 9 Team Members will receive 0.5% (as Passive Income).

Target & Bonanza Rewards

Sales targets for the selling individual (Associate or Member) will be evaluated over a 6-month timeframe. Based on cumulative sales, upgrade the associate/member reward or bonanza prize (ranging from a smartphone to a car), along with a monthly Patrolling Allowance.

Sales Reward Timeframe & Cash Terms

Timeframe: 6 months from designated sales target start. Rewards executed exclusively via 100% Upfront Cash Payment or 100% EMI, at the Company's discretion. No standard employment benefits apply to this offer.

Associate & Team Workflow

Team Building: The Associate (Leader) creates their ID and builds a team of a maximum of 10 Team Members (11 members total per team). Team Members complete KYC through their Associate and receive training regarding projects/commission structures.

Marketing & Site Visit: The team works together generating leads via company-approved brochures/videos and conducts site visits to close deals; the Associate can assist Team Members in closing deals.

Deal Closure: 100% Upfront Cash Registry to the client explained; 100% offer benefits explained. Strict Rule — never accept cash directly from a client; all payment must be made directly into the Company's bank account.

Registration, KYC & Independent Contractor

Associate registration is absolutely free and linked to Aadhar & PAN card KYC verification (mandatory). Every associate is assigned a single ID strictly for their own use — no sharing/duplication permitted. The Associate acts as an independent channel partner, not as a company employee; no standard employment benefits apply.

Commission on Sales & Base Target

An associate earns a 5% commission based on the fixed company-prescribed rate. Base Target: achieving a total sale of 2000 sq. yards over a continuous 72-month (6-year) tenure entitles a 100 sq. yard plot at company rates. Below-target proportion is calculated per the ratio of sq. yards sold against the 2000 sq. yard base.

Patrolling Allowance (Mega Target)

From the date an associate achieves total sales of 2000 sq. yards, the Company provides a patrolling (travel) allowance of ₹12,000/month for a continuous 72-month duration.

Accidental Death & Disability Benefit

In the unfortunate event of an associate's accidental death during active tenure, the Company provides financial assistance of ₹3,00,000 to the nominee, or a registered plot of 50 sq. yards. For accidental disability resulting in physical loss (loss of limbs), the Company provides financial aid ranging ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 based on the condition.

Financial Liability

The Company is not responsible for any cash collected by the associate. The associate is strictly liable for any funds not officially credited to the Company's bank account via a valid company receipt.

Marketing, Confidentiality & Misconduct

Associates must use only company-approved marketing materials and must protect company data, client leads, and trade secrets without sharing them with competitors. Misleading customers, fake commitments, fraud, mis-selling, or forging documents will lead to immediate termination and forfeiture of pending payouts.

Taxation, TDS & Termination

All commissions, allowances and rewards are subject to TDS and applicable statutory tax deductions as per government rules. The Company is entitled to terminate this agreement for misconduct as described above.

Possession, Registry & Jurisdiction

The Company is responsible for handing over possession and registry (Dakhil Kharij / mutation) to the customer as per company rules. All registry and legal expenses will be strictly borne by the customer. In case of any dispute, it shall be settled by mutual arbitration; subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Unnao / Lucknow.
