Security Guidelines
Unique Admin Password

Use a strong, unique password that you don't use on any other administrative or personal account.

Automatic Session Invalidation

Changing your password will automatically end your current active session and require you to sign back in.

Keep Credentials Confidential

MMR Constructions will never ask for your password via email, WhatsApp, or phone call.

If you suspect unauthorized access, contact the SuperAdmin immediately.
