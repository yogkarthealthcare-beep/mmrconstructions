Terms and Conditions of Allotment & Sale

  1. Allotment & Booking: The allotment of the property is purely provisional and is subject to realization of the initial booking amount. The company reserves the right to accept or reject any application without assigning any reason.
  2. Payment Schedule & Liability: Timely payment is the essence of this allotment. The applicant must make payments exactly as per the agreed payment plan. All payments must be deposited directly into the company's official bank account or at the corporate office — funds should never be handed over in cash to any associate or agent.
  3. 100% Upfront Payment Benefit: If the customer pays 100% of the entire property value upfront at the time of purchase, the company will completely bear the registry expenses (registry will be free).
  4. EMI Plan & Registry: If purchasing on installments, a minimum down payment of 25% to 30% of the plot cost must be deposited. In this case, registry expenses will be entirely borne by the customer.
  5. Delayed Payments & Default: If the applicant defaults on installments, interest @ 18% per month will be charged on the delayed amount. If installments remain unpaid beyond the agreed grace period, the company reserves the right to cancel the allotment and re-allot the plot to another customer at the then-prevailing market rate.
  6. Medical Grace Period: If the customer falls severely ill, a grace period of 1 to 3 months for EMI payments may be granted based on verifiable medical conditions.
  7. Disability Benefit: In the event of the customer suffering a permanent disability, the company will grant a 20% discount on the total property value, based on the total payments made till date.
  8. Demise Waiver Benefit: In the unfortunate event of the customer's death, if 50% of the total property value has already been deposited, the company will waive off the remaining 50%; the co-applicant/nominee will only need to pay the registry and associated expenses.
  9. Cancellation Policy: In the event of cancellation by the applicant(s) for any reason, 10% of the Total Property Value (or the booking amount, whichever is lower) will be deducted as administrative charges. The balance will be refunded without interest within 90 days.
  10. Statutory & Development Charges: All extra charges such as Stamp Duty, Registration Fees, GST, development charges, electricity/water connection, and maintenance are not included in the basic sale price and will be borne exclusively by the applicant(s).
  11. Possession & Modifications: Physical possession will be handed over only after the complete receipt of the Total Property Value and all associated charges. The company reserves the right to alter the layout plan or design as per competent authorities' requirements.
  12. Home Loan & Transfer: The company may provide documents required for home loan approvals, but securing the loan rests strictly with the applicant. Transfer of the booking to a third party is allowed only after realizing a certain percentage of the value and paying the applicable transfer charges.
  13. Dispute Resolution & Jurisdiction: Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this transaction shall be settled through mutual arbitration. All legal matters shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Unnao / Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
Note: In case of any discrepancy, the signed physical copy of the Terms & Conditions of Allotment & Sale held by M.M.R. Construction & Developers Pvt. Ltd. shall prevail.
